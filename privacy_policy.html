Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated: October 19, 2026 / 最近更新日期：2026年10月19日

N5 Japanese Grammar Master (N5日語文法大師) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect (我們收集的資訊)

N5 Japanese Grammar Master is primarily designed as an offline educational application. We do NOT require user account registration, and we do NOT collect personally identifiable information (such as your real name, address, phone number, contacts, or location data).

  • Local Study & Quiz Progress: Your quiz answers, bookmark flags, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Advertising & Diagnostic Data: This application utilizes Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and advertising identifiers (AAID/IDFA) to serve relevant advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services (第三方服務政策)

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: For information on how Google collects and processes data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob Privacy Policies.

3. Children's Privacy (兒童隱私權保護)

This application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. The application is designed for Japanese language learners and general audiences.

4. Security (資料安全)

We value your trust in using our application. Since all your study and handbook data is processed offline on your local device, your study history remains secure and private.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy (政策修訂)

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
